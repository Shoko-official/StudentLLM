## 1. Scope

This policy describes how personal data is processed when you use the optional StudentLLM Cloud service (the "Service"). It covers account sign-in, the Plus plan, cloud models, cloud transcription and OCR, and encrypted backup and sync.

It does not cover the local StudentLLM application. In local mode the application makes no implicit network request, keeps your course data on your device, and does not send it to us. Using the application with your own provider key is a direct relationship between you and that provider, which has its own policy.

The Service is off by default. Nothing is sent to it unless you sign in and turn on a cloud feature after seeing the consent screen.

## 3. Data we process

### 3.1 Account and sign-in data

- an account identifier generated by us (random, opaque);
- your verified email address and, if you sign in with Google, your Google account subject identifier. We request only the basic identity scopes (`openid` and `email`). We do not store a password;
- sign-in security data: refresh-token records (hashed), device labels for the sessions you open, and security events such as failed sign-in counts.

### 3.2 Plan and billing data

- your plan, plan period and status;
- the customer and subscription identifiers assigned by Paddle, our merchant of record.

We do not receive or store your card number or billing address. Paddle collects those as the seller (see section 5).

### 3.3 Usage data

- usage counters (credits used per billing period, number of requests, per-feature counters);
- timestamps (account creation, last sign-in, last request, consent version and time).

These contain no course content.

### 3.4 Content in transit

When you use a cloud feature, the content needed to produce the result is processed: your question and the course passages used as context, the transcript or document text to be formatted, audio for transcription, or an image for text recognition.

This content is processed only to provide the answer you requested. It passes through our gateway to the inference provider and back to you. **We do not store it, we do not log it, and we do not use it to train models.** The inference provider receives it to generate the result (see section 5 for its role and retention).

### 3.5 Encrypted backup

If you turn on encrypted backup and sync, your files are encrypted on your device before upload. We store the resulting ciphertext and its size and timestamps. We cannot decrypt it and cannot recover it if you lose your recovery secret. Ciphertext linked to your account is treated as personal data.

### 3.6 Technical data

Our hosting and network provider and our gateway necessarily process IP addresses and connection metadata (for example timestamps, request sizes, status codes) to deliver the Service, apply rate limits, and protect against abuse. Operational logs hold a request identifier, your account identifier, the route, a status code, a credit count, a latency and a timestamp. They do not hold content or tokens.

### 3.7 Communications

If you contact us we process your message and email address to answer.

### 3.8 What we do not do

We do not sell personal data. We do not show advertising and do not use advertising trackers. We do not profile you or take decisions that produce legal effects about you by automated means. We do not use cookies in the application; if our website uses any, they are described in [PLACEHOLDER: cookie notice].

## 8. Security

We apply measures appropriate to the risk, including: encrypted connections; no content in logs; short-lived access tokens and rotating, revocable refresh tokens; secrets kept only in server secret stores; fixed upstream endpoints; per-account limits; minimal data collection; client-side encryption of backups; restricted access to our systems; and incident handling. In case of a personal data breach likely to create a risk, we will notify the competent authority within 72 hours and tell you where required. Report a vulnerability as described in [SECURITY.md](../../SECURITY.md).

## 9. Your rights

You have the right to: access your data; have it rectified; have it erased; restrict processing; object to processing based on legitimate interests; receive your data in a portable format; withdraw consent at any time without affecting earlier processing; and give instructions about the fate of your data after your death (French law). Cloud features can be turned off in the application, and your account can be closed from the account screen.

To exercise a right, contact [PLACEHOLDER: privacy contact]. We reply within one month, extendable under the law, and may ask you to verify your identity. Because we do not keep your content, we cannot return it; it remains on your device.

You can complain to the French data protection authority, the CNIL (cnil.fr), or to the authority of your country of residence.
